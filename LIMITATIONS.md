# Prototype 2 Limitations

Prototype 2 was intentionally limited in scope in order to isolate and evaluate pressure sensor behavior before investing additional effort into full system integration. Several known limitations affected the realism and completeness of testing during this iteration.

## Mechanical Limitations

- No automated mechanical tightening or load regulation system was implemented.
- The enclosure did not replicate the full mechanical coupling or deformation behavior expected in a deployed tourniquet system.
- Pressure application methods were simplified and did not fully represent real-world tourniquet loading conditions.

## Sensing and Validation Limitations

- Sensor performance was evaluated only under controlled bench-level conditions.
- Testing conditions introduced less variability than would be expected during real-world use.
- The selected pressure sensor lacked the operational range and resolution necessary for reliable interpretation during evaluation.
- Validation was limited to exploratory feasibility testing and did not include physiological, clinical, or human-subject evaluation.

## Scope Constraints

Prototype 2 was intended primarily as an exploratory sensing evaluation rather than a complete medical-assistive device prototype. These limitations were accepted as part of the project objective to evaluate sensing feasibility prior to further architectural development.
